Holistic Development & Activities

We believe in nurturing skills outside the standard classroom environment through vibrant lifestyle programs and student collectives.

  • Placement Cell: Fosters strong institutional relationships with business industries to help graduates secure employment opportunities.
  • College Library: Provides a quiet study environment with access to expansive reading materials, journals, and reference textbooks.
  • Hostel & Campus Life: Offers secure, collaborative boarding facilities ensuring inclusive peer community settings for remote students.
  • Cultural Events: Annual celebrations emphasizing historical values, arts, and mutual ethical community principles.
